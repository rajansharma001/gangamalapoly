Preparing for Your Monthly Senior Cardiologist Consultation in Surkhet

Home / Health Blog / Article
Nov 15, 2026 Dr. Amrit Bogati (MBBS, MD) Cardiology

Patients across Surkhet, Dailekh, Jajarkot, and Kalikot no longer need to travel to Kathmandu for specialist cardiac follow-ups. Every third Saturday of the Nepali (B.S.) month, Senior Cardiologist Dr. Amrit Bogati from Shahid Gangalal National Heart Centre visits Gangamala Polyclinic Pvt. Ltd. on Jumla Road, Birendranagar-6.

1. Bring Your Complete Medication List

Carry your current prescriptions or medicine strips so your cardiologist can review exact dosages for blood pressure, cholesterol, or cardiac medications.

2. Complete Baseline ECG & Blood Tests Early

Gangamala Polyclinic provides daily on-site 12-Lead ECG and well-equipped Haematology & Biochemistry laboratory tests. Completing your ECG, fasting blood sugar, and lipid profile ahead of your slot gives your cardiologist clear diagnostic data immediately.

3. Reserve Your 3rd Saturday Token in Advance

Because monthly cardiology tokens are in high demand across Karnali Province, call reception at +977 83-520611 or reserve online through our Appointment Page.
